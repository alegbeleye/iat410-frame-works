Playtest Runs: Procedure, Observations, and Metrics

2026-02-03 - 11:00

I ran three playtest sessions with seven participants using a standardized procedure: a 3-minute run with fixed starting fuel and randomized flyer spawns. I recorded completion rate, flyers collected, and fuel remaining, and took live notes for qualitative observations. Two dominant strategies emerged: route-first players prioritized reaching the safehouse, while flyer-first players took detours and sometimes failed due to fuel depletion, but produced more interesting emergent moments. Participants consistently struggled to locate the gas station on initial runs, indicating minimap landmarking is insufficient. Pickup feedback (visual/audio) was too subtle - players often missed confirmations that a flyer was collected. The Playtest Report contains session logs, transcripts, and prioritized fixes that we used to decide next steps.
