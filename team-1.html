Team Formation and Project Intent

2026-01-20 - 10:00 AM

Frame Works began as a one-person team aiming to deliver a tightly-scoped prototype rather than a broad, unfinished game. The design intent was deliberately narrow: build a repeatable, short session (2-4 minutes) in which decisions about route and resource management produce meaningful tradeoffs. I articulated three pillars in the initial GDD: 1) locomotion fidelity - make driving feel intentional and reproducible; 2) resource tension - fuel levels and a single gas station create risk-reward decisions; 3) objective clarity - use simple, visible goals like flyer distribution and reaching a safehouse so players immediately understand the win condition. Choosing a small scope allowed focused iteration and made the prototype easy to playtest and document.
