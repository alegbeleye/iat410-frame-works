Implementing Core Systems: Driving, Fuel, and Interaction

2026-01-28 - 14:15

Implementation began with deterministic, tunable vehicle parameters. I iterated acceleration curves, drag, and turning sensitivity using Rigidbody2D settings so that player inputs produced predictable outcomes. For fuel, I tied consumption to throttle and time, but smoothed the UI readout to avoid sudden jumps - this made the player feel the tension without the HUD feeling noisy. The gas station interaction is a simple state-machine: approach, dock (snap-to), refuel (timed), exit. That sequence was kept short so refueling becomes a tactical choice rather than a long interruption. The minimap uses a simple top-down icon layer; initial development prioritized icon clarity (high contrast symbols) because early user tests need immediate recognition of the gas station and safehouse locations. All of these engineering choices are documented and justified in the GDD to make future tuning straightforward.
